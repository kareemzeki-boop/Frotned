Method and evidence notes

ISO 6946:2017 official scope excludes windows, curtain walling, ground-contact components, and designed air permeation. Air cavities use explicit depth, emissivity, heat-flow direction, and ventilation class. A well-ventilated cavity excludes the cavity and layers exterior to it from clear-field resistance while retaining them visually.

Al Sa’fat January 2023, §501.01 / Table 501.01(1): external wall and exposed floor limits are Silver 0.57 and Golden/Platinum 0.42 W/m²K. This rule is only assessed for its stated scope. DMT Estidama Bulletin #8 documents prescriptive and whole-building performance routes; one wall U-value cannot award a Pearl rating.
